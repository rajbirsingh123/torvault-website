Manufacturing

Designed for Canada. Built to reach it.

Torvault safes are engineered to Canadian security and fire-testing conventions, then manufactured at scale in Vietnam — giving Canadian dealers a certified alternative at a materially different price point than domestic-only production.

The Process

Six stages, one spec sheet.

Every Torvault unit moves through the same sequence, from initial engineering to the dealer’s loading dock — each stage checked against the UL testing methodology the finished safe is built to meet.

01

Design & Engineering

Body, door, and locking geometry specified against UL 72 and UL TL testing methodology before a single unit is tooled — every collection is designed to a target rating, not adapted after the fact.

02

Steel Forming & Body Construction

Solid steel plate is cut, formed, and welded into the body and door blank at the thickness specified for each collection — from 12-gauge RSC bodies to 1.5″ solid TL‑30 construction.

03

Hardplate Lamination

Composite hardplate is bonded over the lock and boltwork — and across all six sides on TL‑30 units — to defeat drilling and cutting attacks during UL-methodology testing.

04

Relocker & Locking System Assembly

Spring-loaded relocking bolts, boltwork, and the electronic or mechanical locking mechanism are fitted and function-tested before the door is hung.

05

Compliance & Import Testing

Each collection is verified against its rated standard — fire endurance, forced-entry resistance, and lock function — before Canadian distribution begins.

06

Import & Dealer Distribution

Finished units are imported directly to Canada and shipped to authorized dealer territories — no middle-market distributor step, and no direct-to-consumer sale.

Quality Commitments

What we won’t compromise on.

UL Methodology First

Ratings are engineered targets from day one, not retrofitted claims after production.

Documented QA at Every Stage

Steel gauge, weld integrity, hardplate placement, and lock function are checked before a unit moves to the next stage.

Engineered for Canadian Climate

Finish and hardware are specified for the humidity and temperature range Canadian dealers actually install into.
