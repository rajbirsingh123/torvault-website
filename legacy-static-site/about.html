About Torvault

A new line, backed by decades of experience.

Torvault is a new high-security safe brand entering the Canadian market in 2026 — built by the team behind Security Lock & Safe Inc., a GTA safe and vault door provider since 1987.

Our Story

Why we built Torvault.

Security Lock & Safe Inc. has spent 39 years selling, servicing, and installing safes and vault doors across the Greater Toronto Area under the Toronto Safes name — working with homes, retail chains, and institutional clients including government offices, hospitals, and law enforcement agencies.

That work gave us a direct view into a gap in the Canadian market: independent safe dealers outside our own retail footprint had few UL-certified options that weren’t either imported at retail markup or sold direct-to-consumer by the manufacturer, competing with the dealers who were supposed to sell it.

Torvault is our answer — a manufacturing brand, not a retail one. We engineer to UL testing methodology, manufacture at scale in Vietnam, and sell exclusively through independent Canadian dealers at wholesale pricing with territory protection.

Timeline

From a Concord locksmith counter to a national line.

1987–2025

Toronto Safes grows into a trusted regional name

Nearly four decades supplying and installing safes and vault doors for residential, retail, industrial, and institutional clients — including government, hospital, and law enforcement accounts — plus emergency lockout and locksmithing services across the GTA.

2026

Torvault launches

The same team enters manufacturing with a dealer-first, UL-tested line built for independent Canadian safe dealers — not for direct-to-consumer retail.

How We’re Different

Built for the dealer channel.

  • 01

    Dealer-First Model

    Wholesale pricing and territory protection built for independent safe and security dealers, not direct-to-consumer retail. Torvault never sells direct — every unit moves through an authorized dealer.

  • 02

    Documented UL Testing

    Every collection is built and verified against UL 72, UL RSC, and UL TL testing methodology before it ships — see the full breakdown.

  • 03

    Direct Import Pricing

    Manufactured at scale in Vietnam and imported directly — no middle-market distributor markup passed on to your customers.

  • 04

    39 Years of Industry Backing

    Supported by Security Lock & Safe Inc.’s decades of sales, service, and delivery experience in the Canadian safe market — we know what dealers actually need from a supplier.
